Evolution API alternatives: WhatsApp API providers compared

Evolution API is a capable, actively-developed open-source WhatsApp gateway — Apache 2.0 licensed, free, and it goes further than most of its self-hosted peers on group management and instance handling. If your team already runs infrastructure and wants a full-featured base to build on, it's a solid choice.

The cost is in what "self-hosted" actually means here: running Evolution API in production needs PostgreSQL for session state, Redis for caching, and a persistent volume for your WhatsApp auth data — meaningfully more moving parts than a lighter self-hosted option like WAHA, let alone a hosted service you don't run at all. You're also responsible for patching it when WhatsApp's Web protocol changes, and for any ban-risk mitigation (dedicated IPs, rate limiting) yourself — none of that comes built in. One small but real license detail: Evolution API's Apache 2.0 license requires you to display a visible notice that you're using it, wherever your system is administered — a minor obligation, but a real one, unlike a fully permissive license with no such requirement.

It reads and responds to incoming messages via webhooks — a real capability, not every WhatsApp API provider has it.

So which one should you actually use?

Depends entirely on what you need. Here's a straight answer for the most common cases.

Building a chatbot

For one that only replies to inbound messages (never initiates), the official WhatsApp Business API, direct with Meta, is actually the strongest pick: replies within the 24-hour customer-care window are unlimited and free, so zero cost and zero ban risk, with full freeform messaging. Going through a partner adds inbox/tooling convenience, not extra capability — MSG91 is the top-rated pick if you want that. Want it fully self-hosted and free, or can't go through business verification? WAHA or OpenWA rate the same here; pick whichever's license terms you're more comfortable with. The only reason to reach for an unofficial hosted tool like Periskope here is if you need to prototype before/without business verification, or your bot also needs to proactively re-engage someone after 24 hours of silence — otherwise it adds cost and ban risk with nothing to show for it.

Shared inbox / CRM for a support or sales team

MSG91 is the strongest-rated pick here — official backing, low ban/credential risk, real team inbox, and cheaper than the alternatives. Want lower cost still, and are OK trading away official backing and some privacy for it? Periskope is the unofficial, cheaper alternative. Please note, this is a category by itself & there are several providers that just provide inbox/CRM support without exposing APIs. Those providers are not mentioned here. Prominent among them are AiSensy, Wati & Interakt

Messaging pre-created groups

Only need to send into the group, never read replies? Watobot covers this, free. Need to read group messages too? Periskope is the strongest-rated option that supports it. Want low cost and high privacy instead, and don't mind self-hosting? WAHA or OpenWA.

You need the official green checkmark

Only one real option: the official WhatsApp Business API, direct with Meta (the cheapest baseline — every BSP adds something on top), or through a partner like MSG91, the top-rated option, for the added inbox/tooling convenience.

Sending more than ~250 messages a day

That's real business volume — go with the official WhatsApp Business API, direct with Meta for the lowest cost at real volume (no BSP markup or subscription at all), or through MSG91, the top-rated and cheapest BSP option, if you want added tooling. Whatsapp has documented that they allow less than 250 messages on unverified business account, which means that Whatsapp treat that kind of volume as personal usage limit. Unofficial tools, Watobot included, are built for such volumes; pushing serious daily volume through an unofficial connection raises your ban risk significantly.

No API — just sending/reading from the command line

wacli is built exactly for this — a personal terminal tool, not a hosted API.

Only sending messages, and cost or privacy matters

This is what Watobot is built for. Free, hosted, no message cap we've published, zero-knowledge encrypted, with per-account IP-based ban mitigation. If your need matches this description, it's a genuinely good fit — outside of it, one of the options above will serve you better.
