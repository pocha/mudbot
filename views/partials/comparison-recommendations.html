So which one should you actually use?

Depends entirely on what you need. Here's a straight answer for the most common cases.

Building a chatbot

Want a hosted provider, no server to run? Periskope is the strongest-rated hosted option that can read incoming messages. Cost is the priority instead? GreenAPI is cheaper, with a real (if small) free tier. Want it even cheaper, or privacy matters more than convenience? Self-host — WAHA or OpenWA rate the same here; pick whichever's license terms you're more comfortable with.

Shared inbox / CRM for a support or sales team

Wassenger is the strongest-rated pick here — official API, low ban/credential risk, a real team inbox. Want lower cost and are OK trading away some privacy and the official backing for it? Periskope is the cheaper alternative. Please note, this is a category by itself & there are several providers that just provide inbox/CRM support without exposing APIs. Those providers are not mentioned here. Prominent among them are AiSensy, Wati & Interakt

Messaging pre-created groups

Only need to send into the group, never read replies? Watobot covers this, free. Need to read group messages too? Periskope is the strongest-rated option that supports it. Want low cost and high privacy instead, and don't mind self-hosting? WAHA or OpenWA.

You need the official green checkmark

Only one real option: the official WhatsApp Business API, direct with Meta or through a partner like Wassenger.

Sending more than ~250 messages a day

That's real business volume — go with the official WhatsApp Business API (direct, or via a partner like Wassenger). Whatsapp has documented that they allow less than 250 messages on unverified business account, which means that Whatsapp treat that kind of volume as personal usage limit. Unofficial tools, Watobot included, are built for such volumes; pushing serious daily volume through an unofficial connection raises your ban risk significantly.

No API — just sending/reading from the command line

wacli is built exactly for this — a personal terminal tool, not a hosted API.

Only sending messages, and cost or privacy matters

This is what Watobot is built for. Free, hosted, no message cap we've published, zero-knowledge encrypted, with per-account IP-based ban mitigation. If your need matches this description, it's a genuinely good fit — outside of it, one of the options above will serve you better.
