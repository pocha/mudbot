Gallabox alternatives: WhatsApp API providers compared

Gallabox is an official WhatsApp Business Solution Provider built on Meta's Cloud API, with a genuine shared team inbox and chatbot/automation builder included — a complete product, not a bare API. It's also priced well below most competing team-inbox BSPs: plans start around ₹2,399/month (roughly $29) on annual billing, cheaper than comparable options like Wassenger while offering a similar feature set.

The trade-offs are the ones every official-API provider carries: real business verification before you can send anything, pre-approved templates required outside a 24-hour customer-reply window, and no group messaging support at all — a Meta platform limitation, not something Gallabox can work around.
