OpenWA alternative: how Watobot compares

OpenWA (formerly wa-automate) is one of the oldest and most feature-rich unofficial WhatsApp libraries around, built specifically for chatbot developers — event-driven message listening is core to what it does, and it's genuinely good at that job.

Two things worth knowing before you build on it. First, it's source-available under a "Hippocratic + Do Not Harm" license, not a standard permissive one like MIT or Apache — it carries real usage restrictions (excludes organizations in certain industries, includes a human-rights compliance clause, and the licensor can terminate your license with 90 days' notice for violations). It's genuinely open, but it's not the unencumbered "use it for anything" license some other options on this page carry — worth actually reading before committing a business to it. Second, like every self-hosted option here, you're the one running the server, patching it when WhatsApp's protocol changes, and handling ban-risk mitigation yourself — OpenWA's own docs are upfront that it's a fit for personal projects and internal tooling, not something they'd stand behind for regulated industries or large-scale commercial messaging.

It reads and reacts to incoming messages — a real capability Watobot doesn't have.
