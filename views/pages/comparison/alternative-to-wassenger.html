Wassenger alternative: how Watobot compares

Worth being upfront about first: Wassenger isn't really in the same category as most of the other providers on this site. It's a verified Official Meta WhatsApp Business Partner, built on Meta's real Business Cloud API rather than an unofficial WhatsApp Web session — a genuinely different product with a genuinely different risk profile than everything else being compared here, including Watobot.

That officialness is Wassenger's real strength: no automated-ban risk (Meta doesn't detect and silently kill your session the way it can with unofficial tools), a real team inbox with multiple agents on one number, and full support for the official platform's advanced features. The trade-offs that come with the official API apply here too, unavoidably — real business verification before you can send anything, pre-approved templates required for any business-initiated message outside a 24-hour customer reply window, per-message billing on top of Wassenger's own subscription (from €39.90/month), and — a limitation of Meta's platform itself, not Wassenger — no ability to send to WhatsApp groups at all.

If what you actually want is a personal-number, no-approval-process, freeform-messaging tool, Wassenger isn't really competing for that use case — and neither is the comparison below entirely apples-to-apples. It's here because people researching WhatsApp automation options run into it.
