GreenAPI alternative: how Watobot compares

GreenAPI is one of the most established unofficial WhatsApp API providers — a real product, actively maintained, with 50,000+ customers by their own count, and it's the only provider on this page that also sells genuine official WhatsApp Business API (WABA) access alongside its unofficial gateway. It's a legitimate, mature option, not a toy.

Where it's genuinely strong: it reads incoming messages (Watobot doesn't), supports group messaging, and its free "Developer" plan is permanent, not a time-limited trial — you can build and test against it indefinitely.

Where it falls short for a lot of real use cases: that free plan caps you at interacting with only 3 chats — fine for testing a bot, not usable for actually messaging a real customer list. To send more than that, you're on a paid Business plan (~$8/month per instance). It's also closed-source, so you're trusting their infrastructure and their claims about how your session credentials are handled without being able to verify it yourself.
