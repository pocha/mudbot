wacli alternatives: WhatsApp API providers compared

Worth saying plainly first: wacli isn't really a competitor to the other providers on this site in the usual sense. It's an open-source command-line tool for power users to script and search their own personal WhatsApp from a terminal — sync your chat history to a local SQLite database, full-text search across it instantly, send messages via CLI subcommands. It's a personal productivity tool, not a hosted business API.

Judged as what it actually is, it's a well-built, genuinely free, open-source local tool — read/search/sync is core to its design in a way none of the hosted "API providers" on this page really match. But it runs on your own machine, has no hosting or team-inbox story at all, no built-in ban-risk mitigation, and isn't positioned or supported as something you'd put a business on.

We're including it in this comparison series because people researching "WhatsApp automation tools" run into it — not because it's trying to solve the same problem as the hosted and self-hosted providers here.
