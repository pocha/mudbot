MSG91 alternatives: WhatsApp API providers compared

MSG91 is an official WhatsApp Business Solution Provider, well known in India and increasingly beyond it, built on Meta's Cloud API — real business verification, official standing, no automated-ban risk. Unlike Twilio, it ships a genuine built-in chat inbox ("Hello"), so it's a more complete out-of-the-box product rather than a bare API. It's also priced well below most competing BSPs — roughly $6/month per number, with no markup on Meta's own message rates, and that subscription is waived entirely for the first two months.

The trade-offs are the same ones every official-API provider carries: real business verification before you can send anything, pre-approved templates required outside a 24-hour customer-reply window, and no group messaging support at all — a Meta platform limitation that applies regardless of which BSP you use.
